## What's different from before?

The standard treatment is an antibiotic, and the authors say these leave about 70%
of patients unresolved.{{ref:paper}} Matrubials is trying a different kind of drug:
{{term:peptide|peptides}} designed from human milk proteins and aimed at the harmful bacteria. In
lab tests, three of the four peptides left the protective *Lactobacillus* species
they were tested on alive, while still killing the BV-linked bacteria.{{ref:paper}}
The authors call the planned insert "first-in-class."{{ref:paper}}

That is a lab result, not a result in patients.
